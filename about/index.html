About QualMeters

We build water metering systems for a more transparent built environment.

QualMeters Oy was created to help buildings, municipalities and property portfolios move from manual water reading to reliable water intelligence. Our approach combines accurate metering hardware, modern connectivity, cloud software and resident-facing transparency.
